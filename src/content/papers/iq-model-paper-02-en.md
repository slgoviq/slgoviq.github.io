      To unlock a digital safe, a 5-digit PIN (PQRST) must be entered. The digits are from 0 to 9 and follow these rules:

      1. All 5 digits are distinct.

      5. The fourth digit (S) is a prime number equal to the difference between P and Q (S = P - Q).
      6. The last digit (T) is an even number.

      ---

      What is the value of P?
    options:
      - "2"
      - "4"
      - "9"
      - "16"
    correctIndex: 1
    shortMethod: "P is a single digit square > 1. If P = 4: Q = 1 -> R = 5, S = 3 (prime), T = 8 (even). Sum = 4+1+5+3+8 = 21. Thus P = 4."
